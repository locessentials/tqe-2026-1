Executive Summary

Key metrics for each translation condition. Span F1 and Category Agreement (Balanced) reflect annotator agreement, indicating how much the evaluation data can be trusted for that condition. Category Agreement (Balanced) measures how often annotators assigned the same error category to spans they both flagged, combining each annotator's match rate via harmonic mean (F1-style) rather than a chance-corrected coefficient like Cohen's κ — chosen because κ becomes unstable and hard to interpret with the skewed category distributions and small per-condition span counts typical of this data.

Annotator Reliability

Span F1 measures partial overlap agreement between annotator span boundaries. Category Agreement (Balanced) measures agreement on error category labels for spans that overlap between annotators, combined across annotators via harmonic mean (F1-style). Production deployment target for both metrics: ≥0.60. Low reliability in one condition means its quality metrics should be interpreted with caution.
